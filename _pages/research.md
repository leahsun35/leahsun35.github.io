## Computational Tracking of Global Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)

**Researcher · Shanghai · August–October 2025**

- Constructed a high-frequency panel dataset from GDELT 2.0 (GKG) to monitor global discourse on the 17 UN SDGs, processing more than 10 million news items to identify regional variations in development priorities.
- Developed a multi-stage screening pipeline to disentangle overlapping SDG targets and categorize sustainability-related media narratives across more than 100 countries.
- Used GDELT's GCAM measures to quantify the emotional tone of global climate and poverty discourse, analyzing correlations between policy implementation and sentiment shifts.

## Quantifying Global Macro-Sentiment: Linking U.S. Federal Reserve Signals to Chinese Financial Expectations

**Research Assistant · Shanghai · December 2024–February 2025**

- Analyzed the media frequency and sentiment of more than 15 macroeconomic indicators to quantify international spillover effects of U.S. Federal Reserve policy in Chinese financial markets.
- Optimized a BigQuery-to-Python pipeline to extract and normalize decade-long sentiment time series, accounting for linguistic nuances and regional media biases.
- Conducted time-series correlation analysis and vector error correction modeling (VECM) to identify the impact of FOMC signals on Chinese interest rate expectations.
