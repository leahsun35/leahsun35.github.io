I am a master's student in Economics at Shanghai University of Finance and Economics, with graduation expected in June 2027. I received my B.Sc. in Economics from Anhui Normal University in 2022.

My primary research interests are **labor economics**, **family economics**, particularly marriage and fertility, and **political economy**. I am also interested in urban economics and macroeconomics.

My current research examines bride price and marriage markets in China, combining difference-in-differences analysis with a structural marriage-market model. My previous research experience includes using large-scale news data to study global sustainable development discourse and the transmission of U.S. monetary policy signals to Chinese financial expectations.

Read more about my [research]({{ '/research/' | relative_url }}) or view my [CV]({{ '/cv/' | relative_url }}).
